# Variable: China Regional Influence Index

- unit: Index 0–100 (composite of diplomatic relationships, economic leverage, mediation role, intelligence presence)
- related_loops: L-05

## Definition

A composite measure of China's ability to shape Middle East outcomes across military, diplomatic, economic, and informational dimensions. Includes: depth of bilateral economic relationships (trade volume as % of Gulf GDP), diplomatic mediation roles, arms sales penetration, intelligence and surveillance infrastructure, and political alignment in multilateral forums.

## Unit

Index 0–100: components include bilateral trade dependency, diplomatic mediation episodes, SCO/BRI membership, arms sales, and expert assessments.

## Related Loops

L-05 (China Influence Expansion)

## Historical Range

- 2015: ~25 (primarily economic, limited strategic role)
- 2020: ~40 (BRI expansion, growing bilateral depth)
- 2023: ~55 (Saudi-Iran mediation success, SCO expansion, yuan oil payments)
- 2026: ~60–65 (Hormuz closure accelerating Gulf hedging; estimated)
