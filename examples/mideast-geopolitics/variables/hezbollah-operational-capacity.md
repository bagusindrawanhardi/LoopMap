# Variable: Hezbollah Operational Capacity

- unit: Percentage of pre-2024 peak operational capability (0–100%)
- related_loops: L-01, L-06

## Definition

Hezbollah's overall military operational capacity — a composite of fighter numbers and quality, weapons stockpiles (rockets, precision missiles, drones), command-and-control infrastructure, logistics networks, and leadership quality. Expressed as a percentage of its pre-Operation Northern Arrows (Sep 2024) peak capability.

## Unit

Percentage of 2024 pre-war peak: ~100% = Hezbollah at its historical maximum operational strength (prior to Nasrallah assassination).

## Delays

Rebuilding capacity after severe degradation: historical precedent (post-1982, post-2006) suggests 10–15 years to rebuild fully. Partial rebuild to 50% capacity may take 5–7 years.

## Related Loops

L-01 (Proxy Escalation Spiral), L-06 (Hezbollah Collapse-Radicalization)

## Current State (May 2026 estimate)

Estimated at 35–45% of pre-war peak capacity following 2024 degradation and ongoing US-Iran War pressure on Iran funding pipeline.
