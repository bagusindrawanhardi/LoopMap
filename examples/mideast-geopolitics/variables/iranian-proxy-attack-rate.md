# Variable: Iranian Proxy Attack Rate

- unit: Attacks per month (across all proxy groups)
- related_loops: L-01, L-06

## Definition

The combined monthly rate of military attacks conducted by Iran-backed proxy forces (Hezbollah, Houthis, PMF/Iraq militias, Hamas/PIJ, Syrian militias) against US forces, US allies, commercial shipping, and critical infrastructure. Includes rocket/missile strikes, drone attacks, anti-ship operations, and IED attacks.

## Unit

Attacks per month aggregated across all Iran-backed proxy groups. Subcategories: attacks on US forces (Iraq/Syria), attacks on Israel, attacks on commercial shipping (Red Sea), attacks on Saudi/UAE infrastructure.

## Delays

3–12 months lag between funding increase and attack capacity increase (weapons delivery, training). Attack authorization decisions can be immediate once capacity exists.

## Related Loops

L-01 (Proxy Escalation Spiral), L-06 (Hezbollah Collapse-Radicalization)

## Historical Range

- 2021–2022: ~5–15 attacks/month (low-intensity)
- Oct 2023 – Feb 2024: 160+ attacks on US bases alone in 4 months (~40/month)
- 2025: Houthi sustained ~15–20 maritime attacks/month
- Post-Feb 2026 US-Iran War: PMF attacks on US bases in Iraq escalated to 30+/month (E-11)
