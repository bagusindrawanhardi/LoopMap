# Variable: Iran Nuclear Program Acceleration

- unit: Rate of uranium enrichment capacity increase (SWU/year added) and reduction in breakout time (weeks)
- related_loops: L-02

## Definition

The rate at which Iran expands its uranium enrichment capacity (centrifuge installation, higher enrichment levels) and reduces its nuclear breakout time. Measured by IAEA quarterly safeguards reports. Higher acceleration = faster path to weapons-grade material and shorter breakout time.

## Unit

Dual measure: (1) Separative Work Units per year capacity added (centrifuge installation rate); (2) Change in nuclear breakout time (weeks of reduction per month).

## Delays

Technical: centrifuge cascade installation takes 3–6 months; enrichment to 90% (bomb-grade) from 60% (current) takes 1–2 weeks at existing capacity. Political decisions are immediate; technical execution follows.

## Related Loops

L-02 (Nuclear Proliferation Cascade)

## Key Thresholds

- 60% enrichment: Iran's current level as of IAEA Feb 2025 report
- 90% enrichment: weapons-grade (HEU)
- Breakout time as of 2025: ~1–2 weeks (from 60% to sufficient HEU for one weapon)
- Weaponization (bomb design, testing): estimated 6–18 months beyond enrichment
