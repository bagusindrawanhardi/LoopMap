# Variable: Middle East Refugee Displacement

- unit: Millions of displaced persons (forcibly displaced within or across borders)
- related_loops: L-03

## Definition

The total number of persons in the Middle East region who are forcibly displaced from their homes due to conflict, persecution, or state failure — including refugees (crossing international borders), internally displaced persons (IDPs, within their country), and stateless persons. Includes cumulative populations from Syria, Yemen, Iraq, Gaza/Palestine, and new displacements from the US-Iran War 2026.

## Unit

Millions of displaced persons. Regional total as of end-2025: ~35–38 million.

## Delays

Displacement rises immediately (within days/weeks of conflict escalation). Resolution (repatriation, resettlement) takes 10–25 years minimum following civil conflict.

## Related Loops

L-03 (Arab Normalization Reversal)

## Key Host Country Stress Points (UNHCR 2024)

- Jordan: 3M+ refugees out of 11M population (27%+)
- Lebanon: 1.5M Syrians + 500K+ Palestinians = ~25–30% of pre-war population
- Iraq: 1.1M IDP despite end of formal ISIS conflict
- Turkey: 3.6M registered Syrians (world's largest refugee host)
