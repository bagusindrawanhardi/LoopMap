# Mechanism: Nuclear Deterrence Threshold Calculation

- confidence: High

## Mechanism Name

Nuclear Deterrence Threshold Calculation

## Relationship Governed

Iran Conventional Military Degradation → Iran Nuclear Program Acceleration (the mechanism explaining why military pressure accelerates rather than stops the nuclear program)

## Why

States facing existential military pressure exhibit a well-documented pattern of nuclear program acceleration, not abandonment. The causal mechanism operates through regime survival calculus:

1. **Libya-DPRK Lesson** — Gaddafi gave up WMDs in 2003 under US pressure; was killed in 2011. Kim Jong-un retained nuclear weapons; survived. This comparison is explicitly cited by Iranian officials and influences strategic calculations.

2. **Conventional-Nuclear Substitution** — As conventional military deterrence is degraded (air defenses destroyed, missile stockpiles depleted, IRGC infrastructure struck), nuclear deterrence becomes the only reliable substitute. The marginal value of one nuclear weapon increases as conventional forces weaken.

3. **Breakout Time Calculation** — Iran's nuclear program is already so advanced (60% enriched uranium stockpile, IR-6 centrifuges, Fordow hardened facility) that completing the bomb requires weeks, not years. The decision to accelerate carries extremely low incremental cost and extremely high deterrence payoff.

4. **Regime Survival Imperative** — The IRGC, which controls the nuclear program, has institutional interests in its continuation. Nuclear capability is the ultimate IRGC power monopoly — it cannot be delegated to other institutions.

## Boundary Conditions

The mechanism holds when:
- Iran maintains physical nuclear infrastructure (Fordow, Natanz) despite strikes
- IRGC retains political dominance in regime decision-making
- No credible diplomatic pathway offers equivalent security guarantees

The mechanism breaks down when:
- A decisive military strike destroys nuclear infrastructure comprehensively (requires targeting ~12 sites simultaneously)
- A negotiated agreement provides genuine security guarantees + economic relief (new JCPOA++ scenario)
- Regime change brings leadership that prioritizes economic normalization over nuclear deterrence
