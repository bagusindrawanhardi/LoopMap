# Mechanism: Axis of Resistance Strategic Logic

- confidence: High

## Mechanism Name

Axis of Resistance Strategic Logic

## Relationship Governed

Iran Proxy Network Funding → Iranian Proxy Attack Rate (the mechanism explaining why Iran maintains and activates proxy forces)

## Why

Iran's "Axis of Resistance" strategy rests on a doctrine of asymmetric strategic depth: rather than fighting adversaries directly (which would expose Iran's inferior conventional military to US/Israeli overmatch), Iran projects force through proxy groups embedded in neighboring states (Lebanon, Yemen, Iraq, Syria, Gaza). This provides Iran with:

1. **Deniability** — attacks are conducted by nominally independent groups, complicating retaliation authorization
2. **Geographic dispersion** — forcing adversaries to defend simultaneously on multiple fronts across 2,000+ km
3. **Asymmetric cost imposition** — small investments in proxy groups impose costs far exceeding the input (Houthi missiles costing $10K–$100K each disable container ships worth $50–200M)
4. **Political messaging** — proxy activity signals Iranian power and determination to regional audiences and domestic constituencies
5. **Escalation control** — proxy attacks can be calibrated (turned up or down) to signal or negotiate without committing to direct conflict

The mechanism is institutionalized within the IRGC-Quds Force, which maintains permanent advisors embedded with each proxy group, regular weapons transfers, and command structures that can issue attack authorizations while maintaining official deniability.

## Boundary Conditions

This mechanism holds as long as:
- Iran has sufficient financial resources to fund proxies ($500M–$1B/year required)
- Proxy groups maintain autonomous organizational capacity (not fully destroyed by military operations)
- The deniability shield functions (no direct attribution that compels international response against Iran itself)

The mechanism breaks down when:
- Iran faces economic collapse that eliminates proxy funding
- All proxies are simultaneously militarily degraded (coordinated multi-front campaign)
- A clear attribution chain is established compelling response against Iran directly
