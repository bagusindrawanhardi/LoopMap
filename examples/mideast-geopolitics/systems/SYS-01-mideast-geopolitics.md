# System: Middle East Geopolitical Dynamics — US-Iran War Impact

## System Name

Middle East Geopolitical Causal System — Impact of US-Iran War 2026

## System Boundary

**In scope:**
- Iran and its Axis of Resistance proxy network (Hezbollah, Houthis, PMF/Iraq, Hamas/PIJ, Syrian militias)
- United States CENTCOM military posture and deterrence architecture
- Israel security posture and military operations
- Gulf Cooperation Council states (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain)
- Jordan (keystone state and US ally)
- China regional engagement and influence expansion
- Russia-Iran strategic military partnership
- Turkey NATO ambiguity
- Nuclear proliferation dynamics (Iran, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Turkey)
- Refugee and humanitarian displacement
- Arab-Israel normalization (Abraham Accords framework)
- Red Sea / Strait of Hormuz maritime security

**Out of scope (treated as external inputs):**
- Russia-Ukraine war (treated as context, not modeled internally)
- Global oil price dynamics (modeled in separate us-iran-hormuz project)
- Indo-Pacific security competition (treated as external context)
- Domestic US politics (treated as boundary condition)

## Key Stocks

1. Iran Conventional Military Capability (degrades under US/Israeli strikes)
2. Hezbollah Operational Capacity (depleted 2024; slow rebuild)
3. US Military Credibility Index (eroded 2013–2025; reset attempted 2026)
4. China Regional Influence Index (expanding steadily 2015–2026)
5. Saudi Nuclear Threshold Interest (advancing)
6. Middle East Refugee Displacement (accumulating, slow to resolve)
7. Arab-Israel Normalization Progress (oscillating; severely impacted post-Oct 7)

## Key Flows

1. Iranian Proxy Attack Rate (flow of proxy military operations)
2. US Military Response Intensity (flow of US/allied military actions)
3. Iran Nuclear Program Acceleration (flow of centrifuge installation and enrichment level advancement)
4. Gulf State Hedging Rate (flow of diplomatic/economic reorientation toward China)
5. Refugee Displacement Rate (flow of newly displaced persons)

## Time Horizon

Short-term: 2026–2028 (active conflict phase)
Medium-term: 2028–2035 (post-conflict realignment)
Long-term: 2035–2045 (structural change — nuclear order, regional security architecture)

## System Context

The US-Iran War began February 28, 2026 with the Hormuz Strait closure and US military response. As of May 2026, the conflict is ongoing. This causal system models the geopolitical feedback dynamics that will shape the Middle East for the next decade — separate from but closely linked to the economic/energy dynamics modeled in the us-iran-hormuz project.
