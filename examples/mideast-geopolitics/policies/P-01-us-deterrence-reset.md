# Policy: US Deterrence Reset Strategy

## Policy Name

US Deterrence Reset Through Decisive Military Action and Security Architecture Rebuild

## Target Loops

- L-04 (US Deterrence Credibility Cycle) — primary target
- L-01 (Proxy Escalation Spiral) — secondary target (reducing proxy attacks)
- L-05 (China Influence Expansion) — secondary (reassuring Gulf allies)

## Intended Effect

Restore US Military Credibility Index from ~40 to ~70+ by demonstrating unambiguous will and capability to impose decisive costs on Iran and proxies; rebuild Gulf ally confidence through new security architecture commitments; reduce adversary incentive to probe US red lines.

## Mechanism of Effect

1. Decisive military operations against Iranian nuclear sites, IRGC infrastructure, and proxy supply chains that clearly exceed any prior US response
2. Formal security architecture upgrade: US-GCC security treaty with Article 5-equivalent language, or formal basing agreements with explicit defense commitments
3. Extended nuclear deterrence upgrade: publicly committed US nuclear umbrella over Gulf states contingent on non-proliferation compliance, reducing Saudi nuclear threshold interest

## Affected Variables

- US Military Credibility Index (↑ target: raise from ~40 to 65–70)
- Regional Ally Confidence in US Commitments (↑)
- Regional Deterrence Level (↑)
- Iranian Proxy Attack Rate (↓ via deterrence restoration)
- Saudi Nuclear Threshold Interest (↓ via extended deterrence substitution)

## Implementation Delays

3–6 months for deterrence effect on proxy behavior; 12–24 months for ally confidence to structurally rebuild; 24–36 months for new security architecture to be institutionalized.

## Unintended Consequences

1. **Escalation Risk** — Military action against Iranian nuclear sites risks triggering full conventional war with IRGC, closing Hormuz further, activating all proxy networks simultaneously
2. **Nuclear Acceleration Paradox** — Conventional degradation of Iran may accelerate nuclear program (as modeled in L-02)
3. **Arab Street Backlash** — Visible US support for Israeli operations against Iran could reverse remaining normalization, feeding L-03
4. **Russian Counter** — Russia may respond by accelerating weapons transfers to Iran (S-400 upgrades, more Su-35s), partially offsetting US military operations
