# Policy: Iran Grand Bargain — Comprehensive Nuclear and Regional Agreement

## Policy Name

Iran Grand Bargain: Comprehensive Regional Security Agreement Including Nuclear, Proxy, and Economic Dimensions

## Target Loops

- L-02 (Nuclear Proliferation Cascade) — primary target (breaking the loop at Iran nuclear acceleration)
- L-01 (Proxy Escalation Spiral) — primary target (breaking proxy attack authorization)
- L-03 (Arab Normalization Reversal) — secondary (removing Iran as destabilizer)

## Intended Effect

End the proxy escalation spiral and nuclear proliferation cascade through a negotiated framework that provides Iran regime survival guarantees, sanctions relief, and regional security role in exchange for verifiable nuclear limits and proxy force reduction.

## Mechanism of Effect

1. **Nuclear Component**: Iran accepts JCPOA++ limits (60-day breakout time minimum, enhanced IAEA verification including snap inspections) in exchange for comprehensive sanctions relief (~$100B+ in unfrozen assets + oil export restoration)
2. **Regional Security Component**: Iran reduces Quds Force funding to proxies and accepts monitoring; US withdraws offensive military posture from Iran's immediate borders; regional security dialogue including Iran, GCC, Turkey, Iraq, Jordan
3. **Regime Survival Guarantee**: Multilateral commitment (US, EU, Russia, China) to non-regime-change policy; economic normalization creating material stake in stability
4. **Proxy Sequencing**: Iran commits to Houthi ceasefire (allowing Red Sea restoration) as first confidence-building measure; PMF formalization into Iraqi state structure as second step

## Affected Variables

- Iran Nuclear Program Acceleration (↓ primary target)
- Iranian Proxy Attack Rate (↓ primary target)
- Iran Regional Isolation (↓)
- Arab-Israel Normalization Progress (↑ enabled by reduced Iranian destabilization)
- China Regional Influence Index (uncertain — China was grand bargain broker, could increase or stabilize)

## Implementation Delays

6–12 months for negotiation framework; 12–24 months for implementation and verification infrastructure; 5+ years for full economic normalization

## Unintended Consequences

1. **IRGC Resistance** — IRGC hardliners benefit from conflict and sanctions (black market control); grand bargain threatens their institutional interests; implementation risk from IRGC sabotage
2. **Israeli Opposition** — Israel views any agreement that leaves Iran with nuclear latency as existential risk; may take unilateral military action to prevent deal
3. **Saudi Credibility** — A US-Iran deal that doesn't resolve Saudi-Iran rivalry leaves Saudi Arabia exposed; MBS may accelerate nuclear threshold investments as insurance
4. **Precedent Effect** — A deal that rewards Iranian aggression may signal to other states that proxy warfare + nuclear threshold = diplomatic leverage (North Korea model)
