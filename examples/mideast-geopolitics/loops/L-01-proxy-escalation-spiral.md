# Loop L-01: Proxy Escalation Spiral

- loop_type: Reinforcing
- variables: Iranian Proxy Attack Rate -> US Military Response Intensity -> Iran Domestic Nationalism -> Iran Proxy Network Funding -> Iranian Proxy Attack Rate
- narrative: Proxy attacks trigger US military response, which fuels Iranian nationalism and IRGC political dominance, enabling greater proxy funding, which drives further proxy attacks — a self-amplifying escalation spiral
- dominant_period: 2023–2026 (accelerated since Feb 2026 US-Iran War onset)
- delay_points: 1–4 weeks for US military response to proxy attacks; 1–3 months for nationalism to translate to IRGC political consolidation; 3–12 months for funding to translate to increased attack capacity
- leverage_points: Off-ramp negotiations (ceasefire with guaranteed face-saving for Iran); proxy group internal fragmentation; Iran economic collapse forcing proxy funding cuts
- collapse_conditions: Loop breaks if Iran nuclear threshold crossed (US/Israel forced to accept deterrence rather than fight proxy war); or if Iran regime change removes IRGC-proxy command structure; or if all proxies are simultaneously militarily degraded beyond operational capacity

## Narrative

This is the dominant reinforcing loop governing the US-Iran conflict since October 2023. The mechanism operates through four stages:

1. **Proxy Attack** — Houthis attack Red Sea shipping, PMF attacks US bases in Iraq, Hamas attacks Israel, Hezbollah fires rockets into northern Israel
2. **US/Israeli Military Response** — Strikes on proxy infrastructure in Yemen, Iraq, Syria; sanctions; force buildup
3. **Iranian Nationalism Surge** — IRGC frames strikes as "American/Zionist aggression"; suppresses internal dissent using security threat narrative; gains budget and political authority
4. **Increased Proxy Funding and Authorization** — IRGC-Quds Force increases weapons transfers and operational authorization to proxies to demonstrate continued relevance and impose costs

The loop has been spinning continuously since Oct 7, 2023 and accelerated after the US formally declared war on Iran in March 2026. The critical insight is that **each cycle strengthens the IRGC's domestic position**, making de-escalation politically costly for Iranian hardliners.

## Leverage Points

1. **Proxy fragmentation** — Encouraging splits between Iran-directive proxies and locally-driven factions (Hamas political bureau vs. military wing disagreements have been documented)
2. **Off-ramp construction** — Creating a negotiated framework that allows Iran to reduce proxy activity without appearing to capitulate
3. **Proxy financial interdiction** — Targeting IRGC Quds Force financial networks (hawala, cryptocurrency, front companies) to cut the funding-to-attack pipeline
