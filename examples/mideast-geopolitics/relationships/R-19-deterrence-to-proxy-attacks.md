# Relationship: Regional Deterrence Level -> Iranian Proxy Attack Rate

- source: Regional Deterrence Level
- target: Iranian Proxy Attack Rate
- polarity: Negative
- mechanism: Effective deterrence raises the cost calculus for proxy attacks — both by threatening severe punishment and by demonstrating US/allied capability to identify, target, and destroy proxy infrastructure; when deterrence is credible, Iran-backed groups are instructed to maintain operational restraint to avoid triggering escalatory US response that could threaten the regime itself
- delay: 1–3 months (proxy pause after credible demonstration of force)
- confidence: High
- evidence: E-14-us-credibility-deterrence-rand.md — RAND deterrence credibility; E-05-us-centcom-posture-2025.md — US strike operations and proxy response patterns
- counterarguments: After a brief deterrence pause, proxy groups adapt tactics (smaller attacks, deniable operations) and resume at lower intensity — making full deterrence of Iran-backed harassment practically impossible
- related_loops: L-01, L-04
