- source: Arab-Israel Normalization Progress
- target: Iran Regional Isolation
- polarity: Positive
- mechanism: Arab-Israel normalization creates a de facto security-economic bloc aligned against Iran; each new normalization agreement reduces Iran's diplomatic room to maneuver, limits its ability to play Arab states against each other, and strengthens the anti-Iran coalition's ability to impose coordinated pressure (sanctions, intelligence sharing, military coordination); Saudi normalization with Israel would be particularly isolating for Iran as it would unify the two largest regional powers against it
- delay: 6–24 months (isolation effects accumulate through diplomatic and economic channels)
- confidence: High
- evidence: E-06-abraham-accords-fragility.md — normalization strategic implications; E-04-saudi-china-hedging.md — Iranian isolation dynamics
