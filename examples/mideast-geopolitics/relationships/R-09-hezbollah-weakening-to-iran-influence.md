# Relationship: Hezbollah Operational Capacity -> Iran Regional Deterrence Capacity

- source: Hezbollah Operational Capacity
- target: Iran Regional Deterrence Capacity
- polarity: Positive
- mechanism: Hezbollah is Iran's most capable and strategically positioned proxy — holding 150,000+ rockets/missiles targeting Israel, providing Iran a "northern front" threat that has historically deterred Israeli strikes on Iran; as Hezbollah's capacity degrades, Iran loses its primary conventional deterrent against Israeli military action, increasing Iran's vulnerability and reducing its ability to threaten adversaries without direct confrontation
- delay: Immediate (deterrence effect is perceptual, felt continuously)
- confidence: High
- evidence: E-03-hezbollah-degradation-2024.md — IISS Hezbollah degradation assessment; E-01-iran-proxy-network-crs.md — CRS proxy network strategic role
- counterarguments: Even degraded Hezbollah retains significant precision missile capacity (Fateh-110, Zelzal) that can reach Tel Aviv; deterrence is not binary
- related_loops: L-01, L-06
