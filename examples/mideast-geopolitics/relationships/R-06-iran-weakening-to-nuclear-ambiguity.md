- source: Iran Conventional Military Degradation

- mechanism: As Iran's conventional deterrent is degraded by US/Israeli strikes (air defenses, missile stockpiles, IRGC infrastructure), the regime's calculus shifts toward nuclear deterrence as the only reliable protection against regime change; historical precedent (Libya vs North Korea) strongly shapes this logic — nuclear weapons deter invasion, conventional forces do not

## Extended Description

The Libya-DPRK lesson is explicitly cited by Iranian officials. Muammar Gaddafi gave up Libya's WMD program in 2003 under US pressure; he was killed in 2011. Kim Jong-un retained DPRK's nuclear program and remains in power despite crippling sanctions. Iranian strategic planners draw direct lessons from this contrast. The IAEA's February 2025 report showed Iran was already within weeks of breakout capacity — military degradation would remove any remaining restraint on further advancement.
