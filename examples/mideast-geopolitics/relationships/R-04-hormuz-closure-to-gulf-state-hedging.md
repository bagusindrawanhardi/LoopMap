## Extended Description

The Hormuz closure (from Feb 28, 2026) is particularly significant for this dynamic: UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, and Bahrain all depend on Hormuz for oil exports. A prolonged closure forces them to evaluate: does US military presence actually protect their core economic interests? The answer for many Gulf leaders appears to be "not reliably." China's 2023 Saudi-Iran brokered reconciliation is the template — Beijing showed it could achieve what years of US pressure could not (at least temporarily). The 2026 war has accelerated this calculation.
