# Relationship: Iran Regional Isolation -> Iranian Proxy Attack Rate

- source: Iran Regional Isolation
- target: Iranian Proxy Attack Rate
- polarity: Positive
- mechanism: When Iran faces increased regional isolation (diplomatic, economic, military encirclement by US-aligned coalition), the regime responds by activating proxy harassment to impose costs on adversaries, demonstrate continued relevance, and prevent the consolidation of a stable anti-Iran order; isolation removes moderate voices within Iran's foreign policy establishment, leaving IRGC hardliners dominant
- delay: 3–9 months
- confidence: High
- evidence: E-01-iran-proxy-network-crs.md — proxy activation under pressure; E-07-iran-regime-fragility-2025.md — IRGC political dominance during crisis
- counterarguments: At some level of isolation, Iran's proxy funding capacity is genuinely constrained by economic collapse, limiting the feedback loop's strength
- related_loops: L-01, L-03

## Extended Description

The historical pattern is consistent: maximum pressure campaigns against Iran (2018–2020 Trump administration) were followed by dramatically increased proxy activity — Houthi attacks on Saudi Aramco (2019), attacks on US forces in Iraq, seizure of tankers in the Gulf. The mechanism is strategic: proxy harassment is Iran's primary tool for signaling displeasure and negotiating from a position of asymmetric cost imposition when conventional military options are too risky.
