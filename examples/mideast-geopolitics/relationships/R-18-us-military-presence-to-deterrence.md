# Relationship: US Military Presence Level -> Regional Deterrence Level

- source: US Military Presence Level
- target: Regional Deterrence Level
- polarity: Positive
- mechanism: US forward-deployed forces (carrier strike groups, pre-positioned equipment, 40,000+ troops) provide tangible deterrence through three mechanisms: firepower to respond immediately to attack, tripwire effect making US directly implicated in any conflict, and signaling of political will to defend allies; presence enables rapid power projection that distances adversaries cannot match
- delay: Immediate to 1 month (deterrence is a perception effect that adjusts quickly with visible presence)
- confidence: High
- evidence: E-05-us-centcom-posture-2025.md — CENTCOM posture and force levels; E-14-us-credibility-deterrence-rand.md — deterrence effectiveness analysis
- counterarguments: As RAND 2024 found, presence without demonstrated will to use force may not deter — adversaries may call the bluff if they believe US is politically constrained
- related_loops: L-04
