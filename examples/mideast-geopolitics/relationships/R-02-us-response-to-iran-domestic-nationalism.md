# Relationship: US Military Response Intensity -> Iran Domestic Nationalism

- source: US Military Response Intensity
- target: Iran Domestic Nationalism
- polarity: Positive
- mechanism: US/Israeli strikes on Iranian territory or proxies activate a documented "rally-around-the-flag" effect in Iranian domestic politics; regime frames attacks as foreign aggression, suppressing internal opposition and temporarily uniting the population around national defense; IRGC gains political dominance during conflict periods
- delay: Immediate to 2 weeks (public opinion); 1–3 months (political consolidation)
- confidence: High
- evidence: E-07-iran-regime-fragility-2025.md — Iran regime legitimacy dynamics; E-09-russia-iran-military-alignment.md — IRGC political role
- counterarguments: If strikes cause severe civilian casualties and economic collapse simultaneously, the rally effect may be reversed into regime blame (seen partially in 2022 protests post-economic deterioration)
- related_loops: L-01, L-06

## Extended Description

The rally-around-the-flag dynamic is a well-documented cross-national phenomenon. In Iran's case, the IRGC institutionally benefits from external threats — it gains budget, political prestige, and suppression authority. Historical examples: US killing of Qasem Soleimani (Jan 2020) initially sparked massive pro-regime demonstrations despite Soleimani's personal popularity; Israeli strikes on Iranian consulate in Damascus (Apr 2024) triggered Iran's first-ever direct missile/drone attack on Israeli territory, with broad domestic support. The mechanism works most strongly when Iran can credibly claim defensive justification.
