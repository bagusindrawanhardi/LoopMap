# Evidence E-08: Houthi Red Sea Attacks — Maritime Impact

- evidence_type: Report
- source: UNCTAD, "Red Sea Crisis: Assessing the Impact on Trade Flows", Geneva, 2025; supplemented by Lloyd's of London market data Q1 2025
- release_date: 2025-04-10
- retrieved_date: 2026-05-18
- finding: Houthi attacks on commercial shipping in the Red Sea (beginning November 2023) caused 70%+ diversion of container traffic from Suez Canal by early 2025; Red Sea container volumes dropped from ~10.7M TEU/month (2023) to ~2.9M TEU/month (Jan 2025); ships rerouting added ~14,000 km (Cape of Good Hope) per trip, increasing shipping costs 300–500%; war risk insurance premiums for Red Sea transits rose 0.7–1.5% of hull value per voyage (from 0.01% baseline); Houthis claimed 100+ attacks on vessels through March 2025 using anti-ship ballistic missiles, cruise missiles, and attack drones
