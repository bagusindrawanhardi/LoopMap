# Evidence E-10: Middle East Refugee and Displacement Crisis

- evidence_type: Report
- source: UNHCR, "Global Trends: Forced Displacement 2024", Geneva, June 2024; supplemented by IOM Regional Report Q4 2024
- release_date: 2024-06-19
- retrieved_date: 2026-05-18
- finding: As of end-2024, the Middle East hosted 32+ million forcibly displaced persons: Syria (6.8M refugees external, 6.7M IDP); Yemen (4.5M IDP); Gaza (2.2M displaced within 365 km²); Sudan exodus creating secondary displacement into Egypt/Libya; Jordan hosts 770,000 registered Syrian refugees (3M+ estimated total including unregistered = 30% of Jordanian population); Lebanon hosts 1.5M Syrian refugees + 90,000 Palestinian refugees from Gaza = ~26% of total population; Iraq hosts 1.1M IDP; Iran-Iraq War 2026 onset expected to generate 500,000–1.5M additional displacement from Iranian cities under bombardment
- supports_relationships: R-15-refugee-flow-to-jordan-stability.md, R-16-jordan-stability-to-regional-security.md
- supports_loops: L-03
- confidence_boost: High
- quote: "The Middle East represents the world's largest and most protracted displacement crisis — each new conflict layer adds cumulative burden to already overwhelmed host nations."
