# Hypothesis: Iranian Regime Collapse Under Combined Military-Economic Pressure

## Hypothesis Statement

The combination of US military strikes on IRGC infrastructure, Hormuz closure economic self-damage, and accumulated domestic dissatisfaction (from 2022 Women Life Freedom uprising) could reach a tipping point that triggers Iranian regime collapse or fundamental transformation by 2028.

## Supporting Evidence

- E-07: Iran regime fragility indicators (Freedom House/ICG, Mar 2025): 2024 election turnout 41% (lowest since 1979); 2022–2023 protests in all 31 provinces; GDP per capita declined 30% from 2017 peak
- E-02: IAEA reports: sanctions and military pressure have not stopped nuclear program but have severe economic side effects (inflation 60%+ in 2026)
- Historical precedent: US-backed regime change campaigns have occasionally succeeded under military-economic combined pressure (Shah of Iran 1979, though that was against a US ally)

## Conflicting Evidence

- E-05: CENTCOM assessment: IRGC retains strong domestic repression capacity (security apparatus modernized post-2022 protests)
- E-09: Russia-Iran partnership provides economic lifeline (oil swap routes via Russia, yuan payments) reducing isolation effectiveness
- Historical robustness: Islamic Republic has survived: Iran-Iraq War (1980–88), US maximum pressure (2018–2020), multiple waves of domestic protest; regime has demonstrated extraordinary resilience
- Rally-around-flag effect (R-02): External military pressure may strengthen regime by activating nationalism

## Confidence Level

Low — regime collapse is possible but not probable in the 2026–2028 timeframe. More likely: regime stress that increases IRGC internal dominance and nuclear acceleration.

## Variables Involved

- Iran Conventional Military Capability (degrading)
- Iran Domestic Nationalism (ambiguous — could support or undermine regime depending on conflict trajectory)
- Iran Nuclear Program Acceleration (accelerating regardless)
- Iran Proxy Network Funding (constrained but not eliminated)

## Conditions That Would Raise Confidence to Medium

1. Sustained strikes on IRGC leadership specifically (decapitation campaign succeeds beyond Soleimani precedent)
2. Hormuz economic self-damage severely hurts Iranian domestic economy (Iran needs strait for 90%+ of its own energy exports)
3. Khamenei succession crisis materializes (Supreme Leader health crisis)
4. IRGC internal factional split (some evidence of tensions between pragmatists and hardliners)
